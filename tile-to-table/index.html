Practice deck

From glass slide to warehouse table, out loud.

Data engineering where the source data is whole-slide images: SQL over cases and scans, batch and streaming pipelines, orchestration and delivery, analytics and ML, and the lab and imaging plumbing around a slide scanner. Each card asks you to explain, compare, write or debug something, then shows a model answer to check yourself against.

How to use it: answer out loud before you reveal, grade yourself honestly, and come back when cards are due. Ten minutes most days beats an hour once a week.
