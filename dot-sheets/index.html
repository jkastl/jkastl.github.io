    3 Page

    Add a picture to make a page.

    Faces, pets and simple scenes work best. Crop close to the subject: the page boils it down to a handful of flat, bold shapes, like a cartoon of the photo.

    In the print dialog choose your paper size, Scale: 100% (or “Actual size”) and Margins: None or Default. The page already has its own margins. Check that the ruler at the bottom measures 50 mm.

    How it works

    Like the kits

    Dot-by-letter kits such as Lifelines FlowArt print a scene as rows of tiny gray letters. You pick a marker for each letter, color in the key at the top, then cover every letter with a dot: press firmly on big letters and tap lightly on small ones. The picture shows up as you go. This page makes those sheets from your own pictures.

    From photo to cartoon

    The picture is shrunk, its colors are pushed a little brighter, and an edge-preserving filter flattens texture while keeping outlines. The subject gets most of the colors and keeps its small details, and the background is simplified harder. Eyes, noses and edges become a row of dark dots, like the outline of a pet on a kit page. Anything thinner than one dot is merged away, which leaves a handful of flat, bold shapes.

    Your markers or the picture's

    My markers picks the best few colors from the kits you tick, so the page matches what's in your drawer. Match the picture uses whatever colors suit the picture best, and the key suggests the closest marker you own. You can change any letter's color or merge two letters afterwards. Kit colors were sampled from product photos, so they're close, not exact.

    Laying the dots

    Like the kits, the dots run in rows that follow each shape's outline. A row of small dots traces every edge, and bigger dots fill the middles in rings. Letters are sized to the dot they ask for, with a little white space between them so each one reads clearly.

    Full pages and minis

    A full page is one picture on 8½ × 11 in paper, 8 colors, like the full-size kits. 4 minis per sheet fits four 4¼ × 5½ in pages on one sheet, like FlowArt Mini pads: 5 colors, finer dots, about half an hour each. Cut along the dashed lines. Each mini can have its own picture.

    Printing

    The page is drawn as vector shapes and text, so it prints sharp on any printer and stays small enough for an older USB printer to take quickly. The margins are built in. The ruler at the bottom tells you whether the print came out at the right size. Black-and-white printing is the default: the key's circles are left blank for you to test-dot each marker.
